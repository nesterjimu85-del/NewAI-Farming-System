Fall Armyworm (Spodoptera frugiperda)

94% Confidence

Visible windowpane holes and frass in the maize whorl indicate early-stage Fall Armyworm infestation.

Recommended Chemical & Organic Treatments:
  • Apply Emamectin Benzoate or Chlorantraniliprole into the whorl.
  • Organic alternative: Apply clean coarse sand or wood ash into maize funnel.
Safety & Application Guidelines:
Wear full protective clothing. Do not spray during peak wind or heat. Respect 14-day pre-harvest interval.
